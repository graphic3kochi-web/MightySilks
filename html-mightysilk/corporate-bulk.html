Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Bulk and corporate orders start from 10 pieces. For smaller quantities, you're welcome to order directly from our shop.

Can I mix different products in one order?

Yes. You can combine sarees, lehengas and ready-to-wear pieces in different colours and designs within one bulk order, subject to availability.

How long does a bulk order take?

It depends on the quantity and availability of the products you choose. We'll confirm the timeline with your quote, so please contact us as early as possible for festive or wedding dates.

Do you offer gift wrapping and personalisation?

Yes. We can provide gift boxes, wrapping and personalised message cards. Let us know your requirements in the enquiry form.

How do payment and invoicing work?

Payment terms are agreed with your quote. We provide a proper invoice for every corporate order. See our Payment Policy for accepted payment methods.

Can you deliver to multiple addresses?

Yes. We can ship your order to a single location or directly to each recipient. Share the delivery details with us and we'll include shipping in your quote.
