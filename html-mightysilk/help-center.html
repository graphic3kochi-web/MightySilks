Frequently Asked Questions

Orders & Tracking

How do I place an order?

Browse our collections, choose your item and click Add to Cart or Buy Now. Open your cart, select Checkout, enter your contact and delivery details, choose a shipping and payment method, and place your order. You'll see a confirmation straight away.

How can I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched, we'll email you a tracking number and link. You can also follow your order's progress on our Track Your Order page.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, as long as it hasn't been dispatched. Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. Cancellations before dispatch are free and fully refunded. Once dispatched, an order can't be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.

I didn't receive an order confirmation. What should I do?

Please check your spam or promotions folder first. If you still can't find it, contact us with the email address or phone number you used, and we'll confirm your order.

Shipping & Delivery

How much does shipping cost?

Standard Delivery is free on orders of ₹1,999 or more (after any coupon), and ₹99 below that. Express Delivery costs ₹250. See our Shipping Policy for details.

How long will delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days. Standard Delivery then takes 5–7 business days and Express Delivery 2–3 business days. Remote areas and festive seasons may take a little longer.

Do you ship outside India?

We currently ship within India only. If you'd like to order from abroad, please contact us and we'll let you know if we can help.

Can I change my delivery address?

Yes, before your order is dispatched. Contact us with your order number and the new address as soon as possible.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

You can return or exchange eligible items within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed and in their original packaging with all tags attached. Read the full Return Policy.

How do I request a return or exchange?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number, the item and the reason. Once approved, we'll arrange a pickup or share the address to send it to.

Which items can't be returned?

Customised or altered items, sarees with stitched blouses or fall and pico work done at your request, clearance sale items and gift cards can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

My item arrived damaged or wrong. What do I do?

We're sorry! Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos or an unboxing video. We'll arrange a free pickup and send a replacement or a full refund.

Payments & Refunds

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and more), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay) and Cash on Delivery where available. See our Payment Policy.

Is Cash on Delivery available?

Yes, for eligible orders and delivery locations. Choose Cash on Delivery at checkout and keep the exact amount ready when your order arrives.

Money was deducted but my order failed. What now?

Don't worry. Your bank or payment provider usually reverses the amount automatically within 5–7 business days. If it doesn't, contact us with your transaction details.

When will I get my refund?

Approved refunds are processed within 5–10 business days to your original payment method. Cash on Delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer or UPI.

How do I use a coupon code?

Enter your code, such as NEW10, in your cart or at checkout and click Apply. The discount is shown before you pay. One coupon can be used per order.

Account & Login

Do I need an account to shop?

No, you can check out as a guest. An account makes checkout faster and lets you see your orders and wishlist in one place. Create an account.

Can I log in with my phone number?

Yes. On the Log In page, choose Phone Number, enter your number and we'll send you a one-time code to sign in.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click Forgot password? on the Log In page to reset it, or log in with your phone number and a one-time code instead.

Products & Care

Does my saree come with a blouse piece?

Most of our sarees include an unstitched blouse piece. Please check the product description, which says whether a blouse is included.

Why does the colour look slightly different from the photo?

Screen settings and lighting can change how colours appear, and silk and zari shine differently in different light. Handwoven pieces may also have slight natural variations, which make each one unique.

How should I care for my silk saree?
  • Dry clean only, especially for the first few washes.
  • Store it folded in a cotton or muslin cloth, away from direct sunlight.
  • Refold it every few months to prevent creases from setting in the zari.
  • Iron on a low setting with a cloth between the iron and the fabric.
